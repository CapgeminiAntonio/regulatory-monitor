**Version 3.2, effective 2026-01-15.**

## 1. Scope
1.1 Applies to all advertisements, sales literature, correspondence, social media, websites and performance presentations. This includes content created or personalized with AI, under the SEC Marketing Rule (Advisers Act Rule 206(4)-1) and FINRA Rule 2210.

## 2. General standards
2.1 Communications must be fair and balanced, with no untrue or misleading statements. Material claims must be substantiated before use.
2.2 Risks must be presented with prominence comparable to benefits.

## 3. Performance advertising
3.1 Adviser advertisements must show net performance at least as prominently as gross performance, with 1-, 5- and 10-year periods.
3.2 **Hypothetical performance**, including model, back-tested and **projected or targeted returns**, may only be shown to audiences for whom it is relevant to their financial situation. It must be accompanied by the assumptions and risks, and requires CCO pre-approval.
3.3 Broker-dealer retail communications may not project performance or predict future returns except as expressly permitted under FINRA Rule 2210(d)(1)(F).

## 4. Testimonials, endorsements and third-party ratings
4.1 These require clear disclosure of compensation and conflicts, a written agreement for paid promoters above the de minimis threshold, and oversight.

## 5. Review and approval
5.1 Principal approval is required before first use of any retail communication. Filing with FINRA occurs where required.
5.2 AI-generated marketing content must be reviewed by a registered principal before publication.

## 6. Recordkeeping
6.1 All communications and approval evidence are retained under the Books & Records policy.
