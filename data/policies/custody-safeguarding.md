**Version 4.0, effective 2026-02-01. Applies to Northwind Advisors, Northwind Securities and the funds they advise.**

## 1. Scope
1.1 This policy governs how client funds and securities are held and protected when Northwind has custody under Advisers Act Rule 206(4)-2, Investment Company Act Section 17(f), or Exchange Act Rule 15c3-3.

## 2. Qualified custodians
2.1 Client assets must be maintained with qualified custodians (banks, registered broker-dealers, futures commission merchants, or qualifying foreign financial institutions).
2.2 Custodian agreements require account statements to be sent directly to clients at least quarterly.
2.3 The Custody Committee approves every new custodian relationship.

## 3. Surprise examinations and audits
3.1 Where Northwind has custody, an independent public accountant performs an annual surprise examination.
3.2 Private funds satisfy the custody rule through annual audits, with audited financials delivered to investors within 120 days of fiscal year-end.

## 4. Digital assets
4.1 Crypto assets may only be held for clients through custodians approved by the Custody Committee. No proprietary self-custody is permitted.
4.2 Before approval, digital-asset custodians must demonstrate segregation of client assets, private-key controls, and insurance or other loss-protection arrangements.

## 5. Standing letters of authorization and inadvertent custody
5.1 Standing letters of authorization (SLOAs) are reviewed against SEC no-action conditions before acceptance.
5.2 Checks or assets inadvertently received are forwarded to the client or custodian within 3 business days and logged.

## 6. Broker-dealer customer protection
6.1 Northwind Securities does not hold customer funds or securities. Customer accounts are carried by its clearing firm, and the firm operates under the Rule 15c3-3 exemption or the applicable footnote 74 arrangement.

## 7. Review
7.1 Operations reconciles custodial positions daily. Compliance tests custody controls annually.
