**Version 6.0, effective 2026-01-01.**

## 1. Scope
1.1 Implements the Bank Secrecy Act and FinCEN regulations for broker-dealers and investment advisers, FINRA Rule 3310, and OFAC sanctions requirements across all Northwind entities.

## 2. Program elements
2.1 Written policies, an independent annual test, a designated AML Compliance Officer, and annual training.
2.2 The program is approved by senior management and reviewed annually and upon regulatory change.

## 3. Customer identification and due diligence
3.1 The Customer Identification Program (CIP) verifies identity before account opening.
3.2 Beneficial owners of legal-entity customers are identified and verified at the 25% ownership and control-prong thresholds.
3.3 Enhanced due diligence applies to high-risk clients, including **cross-border Latin American private clients**, PEPs, and accounts funded from higher-risk jurisdictions.

## 4. Monitoring and reporting
4.1 Transaction monitoring covers wires, journals, low-priced securities and digital-asset-related flows.
4.2 Suspicious Activity Reports are filed within 30 days of initial detection.
4.3 Funds transfers at or above the **Recordkeeping and Travel Rule threshold** must collect, retain and transmit the required originator and beneficiary information.
4.4 Currency Transaction Reports are filed where applicable.

## 5. Sanctions
5.1 Clients and counterparties are screened against OFAC lists at onboarding and daily.
5.2 Transactions involving jurisdictions or entities subject to **FinCEN special measures (Section 311 / 9714)** are blocked or rejected.

## 6. Information sharing
6.1 Northwind participates in Section 314(a) requests and 314(b) voluntary information sharing.
