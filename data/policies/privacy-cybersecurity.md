**Version 4.2, effective 2026-02-15.**

## 1. Scope
1.1 Implements Regulation S-P (safeguarding customer information and privacy notices), Regulation S-ID (identity theft red flags), GLBA, applicable US state privacy and breach laws, and Canadian and Latin American privacy laws for cross-border clients.

## 2. Safeguards
2.1 A written information security program covers access control, encryption at rest and in transit, MFA, vulnerability management and annual penetration testing.
2.2 Customer information is disposed of securely at the end of its retention period.

## 3. Privacy notices and opt-outs
3.1 Initial and annual privacy notices are delivered where required, and opt-outs for sharing with non-affiliated third parties are honored.

## 4. Incident response
4.1 Suspected incidents are reported to the Security Operations Center immediately and triaged within 4 hours.
4.2 **Customer notification:** affected individuals are notified as soon as practicable, and **no later than 30 days**, after unauthorized access to or use of sensitive customer information is detected, as required by amended Regulation S-P.
4.3 Service providers must notify Northwind of a breach within 72 hours (see AI, Technology & Third-Party Risk policy).
4.4 Regulators and law enforcement are notified as required by law.

## 5. Identity theft prevention
5.1 Red flags are monitored for covered accounts, including account-takeover and synthetic-identity patterns.

## 6. Testing and governance
6.1 Tabletop exercises are held twice a year. The Board receives a cybersecurity report quarterly.
