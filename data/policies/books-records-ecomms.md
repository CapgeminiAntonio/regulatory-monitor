**Version 3.0, effective 2026-03-01.**

## 1. Scope
1.1 Implements recordkeeping under Advisers Act Rule 204-2, Exchange Act Rules 17a-3 and 17a-4, Investment Company Act Rule 31a-1, and FINRA Rules 3110 and 4511.

## 2. Retention
2.1 Required records are kept for at least 6 years (3 years for certain broker-dealer records, as specified in Rule 17a-4), the first 2 years in an easily accessible place.
2.2 Electronic records are kept on a compliant system that meets Rule 17a-4(f): either an audit-trail alternative or non-rewriteable, non-erasable storage.
2.3 A designated third party undertaking is maintained where required.

## 3. Electronic communications
3.1 Business communications may only take place on approved, captured channels: email, approved messaging apps, and the approved collaboration platform.
3.2 **Off-channel communications** (personal texting, unapproved apps) for business are prohibited. Employees certify compliance quarterly, and violations are subject to discipline.
3.3 AI meeting summaries and chatbot transcripts relating to the business are treated as business records.

## 4. Supervision and review
4.1 E-communications are surveilled using lexicon- and AI-based review. Alerts are dispositioned within 10 business days.

## 5. Production
5.1 Records must be producible to the SEC or FINRA promptly upon request.
