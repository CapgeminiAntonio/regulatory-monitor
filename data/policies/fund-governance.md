**Version 2.8, effective 2026-04-01.**

## 1. Scope
1.1 Applies to Northwind's registered funds (mutual funds, ETFs, interval fund) and private funds, covering Board oversight, valuation, liquidity and product structures.

## 2. Board oversight
2.1 The fund Board, with a majority of independent directors, approves advisory contracts annually (Section 15(c)) and oversees compliance under Rule 38a-1.

## 3. Valuation
3.1 The adviser is the Rule 2a-5 valuation designee. Fair-value methodologies are tested and reported to the Board quarterly.

## 4. Liquidity
4.1 The liquidity risk management program under Rule 22e-4 classifies holdings, sets a highly liquid investment minimum, and caps illiquid investments at 15%.

## 5. Interval fund and share classes
5.1 The interval fund operates under Rule 23c-3, with periodic repurchase offers of 5–25% of shares and repurchase-pricing deadlines.
5.2 New share classes, multi-class structures and exemptive-relief applications require Board approval and Legal review.
5.3 Retail access to private-market strategies through registered funds is subject to product-committee review.

## 6. Private funds
6.1 Private fund investors receive audited financials, and fee and expense disclosures are reviewed by Legal.
6.2 Preferential treatment of investors (side letters) is disclosed as required.

## 7. Fees
7.1 Fund fee changes and performance-based fees require Board approval and shareholder approval where required by Section 15.
