**Version 3.3, effective 2025-11-01.**

## 1. Scope
1.1 Covers order handling and execution for advisory accounts, funds and brokerage customers, including FINRA Rule 5310 (best execution) and the adviser's fiduciary duty to seek best execution.

## 2. Best execution
2.1 The Best Execution Committee reviews execution quality, broker selection and soft-dollar arrangements (Section 28(e)) quarterly.
2.2 Routing of retail orders is reviewed at least quarterly against competing markets.

## 3. Aggregation and allocation
3.1 Orders are aggregated only where fair to all participating accounts. Allocations are made pro rata or under a pre-approved methodology, documented before execution.
3.2 New issues and IPOs are allocated in line with FINRA Rules 5130 and 5131 eligibility restrictions.

## 4. Cross trades and principal trades
4.1 **Cross trades** between funds or client accounts are permitted only under Investment Company Act Rule 17a-7 procedures for funds. They require an independent current market price, no brokerage commission, and quarterly Board reporting.
4.2 Principal trades with advisory clients require transaction-by-transaction written disclosure and consent (Advisers Act Section 206(3)).

## 5. Trade errors
5.1 Trade errors are corrected at no cost to clients. Gains are retained by the client where required.

## 6. Market access and surveillance
6.1 Pre-trade controls follow Rule 15c3-5. Post-trade surveillance covers manipulation and front-running.
