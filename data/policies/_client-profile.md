# Client profile (fictional): Northwind Wealth & Asset Management

Northwind Wealth & Asset Management is a **fictional** client created for this demo. Any resemblance to a real company is coincidental.

- Privately held US financial services firm headquartered in New York. It is **not** a public reporting company, so SEC issuer-disclosure rules (10-K, proxy, executive compensation) apply only where they affect the funds it advises.
- **Northwind Advisors LLC**: SEC-registered investment adviser (~$85bn AUM) providing discretionary wealth management, model portfolios and sub-advisory services.
- **Northwind Securities LLC**: FINRA-member broker-dealer (dually registered reps), retail brokerage and fund distribution. It introduces accounts to a third-party clearing firm.
- Sponsors and advises **registered funds**: mutual funds, ETFs and one interval fund. It also advises **private funds** (private credit, real estate) offered to accredited investors.
- Clients across the **Americas**: US retail and high-net-worth clients, US institutions, and a growing book of Latin American and Canadian private clients (cross-border accounts).
- Client assets are held at qualified custodians; offers spot crypto ETP exposure and is evaluating direct digital-asset custody through a third-party custodian.
- Uses AI for portfolio-construction analytics, a client-service chatbot, marketing personalization and surveillance (trade and e-comms review). Approximately 3,200 employees and 250 critical vendors.
