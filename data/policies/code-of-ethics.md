**Version 5.1, effective 2025-10-01.**

## 1. Scope
1.1 Implements Advisers Act Rule 204A-1, Investment Company Act Rule 17j-1, and FINRA Rule 3210 for all access persons and registered representatives.

## 2. Standards of business conduct
2.1 Employees must place client interests first and comply with federal securities laws.

## 3. Personal trading
3.1 Access persons pre-clear trades in reportable securities, including IPOs and private placements.
3.2 Holdings reports are filed within 10 days of becoming an access person and annually. Transaction reports are filed quarterly within 30 days.
3.3 Blackout periods apply around client and fund trades. A 30-day holding period applies.
3.4 Crypto assets and crypto ETPs are reportable and subject to pre-clearance.

## 4. Gifts, entertainment and outside activities
4.1 Gifts are limited to $300 per person per year (FINRA Rule 3220), and all gifts are logged.
4.2 Outside business activities and private securities transactions require prior written approval (FINRA Rules 3270 and 3280).

## 5. Material non-public information
5.1 Insider-trading procedures and information barriers apply to the private credit team.

## 6. Reporting violations
6.1 Violations are reported to the CCO. Whistleblowers are protected, and nothing in this policy restricts communication with regulators.
