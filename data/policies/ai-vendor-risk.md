**Version 1.6, effective 2026-05-01.**

## 1. Scope
1.1 Governs AI and machine-learning tools (including generative AI) and third-party service providers that support client-facing or regulated activities. This covers vendors, sub-advisers, the clearing firm and fund service providers.

## 2. AI governance
2.1 All AI use cases are registered in the AI inventory and risk-rated before production use.
2.2 Client-facing and advice-related AI (chatbot, portfolio analytics) requires model validation, human oversight, testing for accuracy and bias, and conflicts review under Best Interest policy §6.1.
2.3 Statements to clients or regulators about AI capabilities must be accurate and substantiated. **"AI washing" is prohibited.**
2.4 Confidential client data may only be used with approved AI tools covered by contractual data protections.

## 3. Third-party due diligence
3.1 Critical vendors receive security (SOC 2 Type II), financial, business-continuity and privacy assessments before contracting, and annually thereafter.
3.2 Contracts require incident notification within 72 hours, audit rights, data-return and exit provisions, and sub-processor approval.

## 4. Outsourcing of covered functions
4.1 Outsourcing a function necessary to provide advisory or brokerage services requires a documented assessment of the provider's ability to perform it and of the risks of doing so. Ongoing monitoring is required.

## 5. Business continuity
5.1 Business continuity plans (FINRA Rule 4370) are tested annually and include vendor-outage scenarios.

## 6. Concentration risk
6.1 Concentration risk to cloud, AI-model and clearing providers is reported to the Risk Committee quarterly.
