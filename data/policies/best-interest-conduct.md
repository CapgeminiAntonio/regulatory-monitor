**Version 2.5, effective 2025-12-01.**

## 1. Scope
1.1 Implements the adviser fiduciary duty, Regulation Best Interest (Reg BI) for broker-dealer recommendations to retail customers, Form CRS, and the conflicts-of-interest program across both entities.

## 2. Standard of conduct
2.1 Advisers act in clients' best interest at all times (duty of care and duty of loyalty).
2.2 Broker-dealer recommendations to retail customers must satisfy the Reg BI disclosure, care, conflict-of-interest and compliance obligations.

## 3. Recommendations and product due diligence
3.1 Complex or illiquid products, including interval funds, private-market strategies, leveraged ETFs and crypto ETPs, require enhanced suitability documentation and product-committee approval before they can be recommended to retail customers.
3.2 Account-type recommendations (brokerage vs. advisory, rollovers) must be documented with a reasonable-basis analysis.

## 4. Conflicts of interest
4.1 Conflicts are identified annually in the conflicts inventory and are eliminated, mitigated or disclosed.
4.2 Sales contests and quotas tied to specific securities within a limited period are prohibited.
4.3 Performance-based fees may only be charged to "qualified clients" under Rule 205-3.

## 5. Disclosure
5.1 Form CRS is delivered to retail investors at or before the earliest required time and updated within 30 days of a material change.
5.2 Form ADV Part 2A and 2B are updated annually and promptly for material changes.

## 6. Use of technology in client interactions
6.1 Predictive analytics or AI tools that optimize, predict or guide investor behavior must be assessed for conflicts before deployment.
